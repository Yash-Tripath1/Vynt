VYNT / PRIVACY

Your camera. Your device.

Policy for VYNT 1.1.0 · September 26, 2026

Camera and microphone

VYNT asks for camera access when you start the camera. With MIC ON, it asks for microphone access when you start recording. The microphone is released when recording ends, including before the save dialog. With MIC OFF, video is silent and VYNT does not request microphone access.

Photos and recordings

Media is processed locally in your browser or desktop app. VYNT does not upload it to a server. Photos and recordings stay temporarily in memory until you save/download them, close the page or close the app. The last-photo preview is not a permanent gallery. Files you save remain on your device until you delete them.

Accounts, analytics and hosting

VYNT has no accounts, advertising, app analytics or tracking cookies. The web hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata, such as IP addresses, to serve the website. Opening GitHub links or downloading the installer involves GitHub's own services and policies. This app does not record computer/system audio.

Control and limitations

You can deny permissions, switch MIC OFF, stop recording, turn the camera off, or revoke access in browser or operating-system privacy settings. Clips are limited to five minutes, with a memory-size safeguard. Closing a recording before saving can lose it. Use camera and microphone features only with the consent of people being recorded.

Questions

Contact the maintainer through the VYNT GitHub repository. Please do not include private photos or recordings in public issues.
